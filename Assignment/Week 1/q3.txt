The before/after contrast (last two rows) is the whole point of lazy binding:
*GOT[printf] flips from 0x401030 (the PLT lazy stub) to 0x00007ffff7c606f0 (the real
printf in libc.so.6) the first time printf is called, after which subsequent calls jump
straight to libc through GOT[printf].
